SEND IT people models
=====================
The bodies, skeleton and skin weights in people.bin / people.json are built from the
MakeHuman base mesh, macro targets and "game_engine" rig published in the MPFB2 project
(https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2). Those assets are released under CC0 1.0
(public domain) by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.

They were rebuilt for the game by tools/people_build.py (shaped into four body types,
clothes regions, a lighter far-away version of each).
